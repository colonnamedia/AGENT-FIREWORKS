# Northline Homes — FireWorks template

Fictional 7-page responsive demo. Original generated hero image and distinct industry workflow. Demo form validates in browser only; it never transmits, stores, books or accepts payment. Demo pages are noindex until actual client facts and compliance needs are reviewed.

## Pages

Home, Buy, Sell, Property Showcase, Neighborhoods, About, Contact. Additional detail routes: none. Reusable hero, service pathway cards, CTA band, industry-specific content, responsive navigation, metadata and contact form.

## Pricing

$1,999 build · $499/year hosting or $59.99/month · $20/year domain. Build reflects 7 pages, customized workflow and client-specific integration planning. Hosting, domain, integrations and third-party fees are separate; annual hosting is primary.

Optional add-ons: IDX/MLS feed and compliance setup from $499 after brokerage/vendor access; CRM integration from $299; additional neighborhood or listing content from $150/page.

## Client inputs

Actual agent and brokerage name, state license, contact, address, service areas, brand assets, approved photos, listing data rights, brokerage disclaimers, fair housing review, IDX vendor and CRM credentials.

## Integrations

None connected. Actual vendor and credentials must be confirmed with the client; end-to-end form delivery, booking and privacy behavior require separate tests before activation.
