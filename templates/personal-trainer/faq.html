FAQ

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Do I need experience before starting?

No. A starter process should meet clients at their current ability and comfort level. Final policies are customized by the trainer.

Can the program support fat loss or muscle gain?

The training can be structured around those goals. Nutrition or medical guidance must stay within the trainer’s verified scope and may require referral to another qualified professional.

Where does training happen?

This demo is configured for Pittsburgh’s South Hills, Canonsburg, and Washington-area searches. Replace it with the trainer’s actual location, travel policy, and online options.

Can existing clients buy more sessions?

Yes. The pricing page can connect to Stripe Payment Links or an existing client-management platform after packages and account credentials are confirmed.

Does this demo form submit information?

No. It validates the fields in the visitor’s browser and clearly confirms that nothing is transmitted, stored, booked, or charged.
