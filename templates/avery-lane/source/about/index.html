01 — ABOUT

Training is personal.
So is the way we begin.

Avery Lane’s relationship with fitness began in the weight room and grew into a career in training, lifestyle coaching, and professional bikini competition. Her original Avery Lane Training story centered on balance, empowerment and individual attention. That foundation still shapes this site’s direction.

Avery also brings a background in counseling, as shared for this project. She knows two people can walk in with the same goal and need entirely different kinds of support. A good plan looks at your experience, confidence, schedule and the life happening outside the gym.

“We start with the person. Then we build the plan.”Proposed positioning for Avery’s review

Whether you want to build muscle, lose weight, regain a routine or prepare for a specific goal, the first step is a conversation about what matters to you.
