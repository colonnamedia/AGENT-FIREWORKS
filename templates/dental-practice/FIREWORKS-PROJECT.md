# Harbor Dental — FireWorks template

Fictional 8-page responsive demo. Original generated hero image and distinct industry workflow. Demo form validates in browser only; it never transmits, stores, books or accepts payment. Demo pages are noindex until actual client facts and compliance needs are reviewed.

## Pages

Home, Services, Our Team, New Patients, Payment & Insurance, Request Visit. Additional detail routes: preventive-care, restorative-care. Reusable hero, service pathway cards, CTA band, industry-specific content, responsive navigation, metadata and contact form.

$2,299 build · $499/year hosting or $59.99/month · $20/year domain. Build reflects 8 pages, customized workflow and client-specific integration planning. Hosting, domain, integrations and third-party fees are separate; annual hosting is primary.

Optional add-ons: Practice booking handoff from $299 after vendor approval; approved insurance information setup from $199; secure intake integration from $499 after privacy/security review; extra service page from $150.

## Client inputs

Actual practice name, address and hours, licensed provider identities, permitted services, approved imagery, accessibility details, verified insurance and payment policies, privacy/consent text and approved scheduling vendor.

## Integrations

None connected. Actual vendor and credentials must be confirmed with the client; end-to-end form delivery, booking and privacy behavior require separate tests before activation.
